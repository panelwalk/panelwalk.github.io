Accessibility statement

Last updated: 13 October 2026. Last checked: 13 October 2026.

We want everyone to be able to use panelwalk.github.io, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader, zoom or high-contrast settings.

Standard

We aim to meet Israeli Standard IS 5568 at level AA, which is based on the W3C's Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG), and WCAG 2.1 at level AA.

What we did

  • Pages use headings, lists and landmarks, so assistive technology can find its way around.
  • A "Skip to content" link comes first, and everything works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
  • Every screenshot has a text description that includes the speech-balloon text shown in it.
  • Text and buttons meet WCAG AA contrast in both the light and dark colour schemes.
  • Each page declares its language and direction, so the Hebrew pages read right to left.
  • Text can be zoomed, and the layout works down to phone width.
  • Nothing moves on its own, nothing has a time limit, and there are no pop-ups or CAPTCHAs. Hover movement is switched off if you've asked your system to reduce motion.

How we checked

Automated checks with axe-core and Lighthouse found no problems on any page. We also went through each page with the keyboard alone, measured colour contrast, and viewed the pages at phone and desktop width in light and dark mode, in Chrome.

Known limitations

  • The site hasn't yet been tested by a person using a screen reader.
  • Our game, The Ice Cream Palace, is hosted on itch.io and isn't covered by this statement. It's a point-and-click game, and we haven't audited its accessibility yet.
  • Sites we link to, such as Instagram, Bluesky and itch.io, are run by other companies, and their accessibility is outside our control.

Report a problem

If something on this site is hard or impossible for you to use, please tell us. Send a direct message to @panelwalkgames on Instagram or Bluesky, and let us know the page and what went wrong. We'll reply and try to fix it.
