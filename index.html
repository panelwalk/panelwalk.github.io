How it works

  1. You send the pages

    High-resolution page files for one arc, about 15–30 minutes of play, plus answers to a few questions about the story.

  2. We build the game

    Every room is one of your panels. We adapt the dialogue into your balloons and turn the story into rooms, clickable objects and puzzles.

  3. You approve it

    You check the build before release. Nothing ships, and none of your art goes into marketing, until you've signed off.

  4. We publish and split

    We publish the game, run the store pages and make the trailer clips. You get 50% of net revenue, paid quarterly with a sales statement.

The deal

  • 50/50 of net game revenue

    Net means what the store pays out after its cut, refunds, payment fees and VAT/sales tax. Paid quarterly with a sales statement.

  • No cost to you

    Nothing, including the sample. No money up front either way, and our work is never recouped from your share. Outside costs (like music or translation) only if we both agree in writing; they come off net revenue before the split.

  • You keep the IP

    Your comic and characters stay yours. We get a non-exclusive licence for this one game, on platforms we agree on (web/itch.io, Steam, mobile).

  • Nothing ships without your OK

    You approve the final build and any marketing that uses your art.

  • Rights come back if we're slow

    If the game isn't published within 6–9 months, the rights revert to you.

  • Your comic comes first

    Comic sales are 100% yours: the game's end screen links to your store, and we never see those sales. The game is never priced below your comic's digital edition. Chapter 1 is free to play in the browser, as promotion for your comic.

Questions

Who owns what?

You own your comic and characters. We get a non-exclusive licence for this one game, for 3–5 years, renewable. We also ask for first option on adapting your next arc or book.

What do you need from me?

High-resolution page files for one arc (about 15–30 minutes of play), answers to a few questions about the story, and a check of the build before release. Sharing the launch with your readers helps a lot, but it isn't required.

How long does it take?

We don't quote a build time yet; we will once we've timed more than one adaptation. What we do commit to: if the game isn't published within 6–9 months, the rights come back to you.

How do I get paid?

Quarterly, with a sales statement. The minimum payout is about $20.

What will players pay?

Chapter 1 is free in the browser. The full game is priced at or above your comic's digital edition. If your comic is print-only or a free webcomic, it's priced like a short game: $6.99–9.99, depending on length.

What if I want out?

If either side leaves, the game comes off sale. Players who already bought it keep their copies.
