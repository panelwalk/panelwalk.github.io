Privacy notice

Last updated: 13 October 2026

In short

  • This website sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device.
  • It has no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no forms.
  • It loads nothing from other companies' servers. Even the font is hosted here.
  • Our host, GitHub Pages, logs visitors' IP addresses for security. We don't see those logs.

Who runs this site

Panelwalk (a working name) runs panelwalk.github.io and is responsible for it. To reach us about privacy, send a direct message to @panelwalkgames on Instagram or Bluesky. We'll add a contact email address to this page. Until then, a DM is the way to reach us.

When you visit

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. GitHub states that when a Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes (GitHub Docs). GitHub is based in the United States and may process this data there. We don't receive these logs and can't see who visits.

The reason for this processing is to deliver the site securely, which is a legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). For details, including how long GitHub keeps logs, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Cookies and storage

None. This site doesn't set cookies or use your browser's local storage, so there is nothing to consent to. Your browser may cache the page's files, as it does for any website.

Links to other sites

We link to Instagram, Bluesky and itch.io, where our game is hosted. Once you follow a link, that site's own privacy policy applies, for example Instagram's, Bluesky's and itch.io's.

Our game The Ice Cream Palace runs on itch.io. It saves your progress and settings in your own browser and sends nothing to us.

If you message us

If you send us a DM, we receive what the platform shows us: your handle, your profile and your message. We use it only to reply to you and to talk about a possible game. If you send comic pages for a free sample, we use them only to make your private sample. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

The reason for this processing is to answer your request and, if you want, to prepare an agreement with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

How long we keep it

We delete your messages and any comic pages you send us 3 months after our conversation ends, unless we agree a deal. If we do, we keep them as long as our agreement requires.

If we make a sample game from your pages, we delete it when you ask, or within the same 3 months if no deal follows.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, to restrict how we use it, or object to its use. Send us a DM to do so.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In the EU, that's usually the authority where you live (list of EU authorities). In Israel, it's the Privacy Protection Authority.

Changes

If the site changes what it collects, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
